What do we do?

We offer a wide range of tracks for MSA University students, including AI & Machine Learning, C++, Web Development, Game Development, and Robotics. Our engaging workshops, events, and competitions are designed not only to enrich skills but also to create lasting memories and build strong connections within our community.

Events▣

Project fair & majors orientation day

IEEE organized the Majors Orientation Day, featuring inspiring talks on engineering disciplines to guide students in choosing their career paths. The event was honored by the presence of University President Prof. Khairy Abdel Hamied and Dr. Nabila, Deputy Vice Dean of the Faculty of Engineering.

IEEE 2024 Recruitment Week

IEEE's 2024 Recruitment Week welcomed an enthusiastic group of young, passionate students, marking the start of memorable journeys together. It was a week filled with energy and excitement, as we connected with new members and began building lasting memories from day one.

Your Journey to the Cloud

IEEE collaborated with industry experts to introduce students to cloud computing. Mohamed Rizk (Cloudkode) covered AWS basics, Mohamed Abumuslim (Microsoft) shared insights on cloud auditing, George Fahmy (Stakpak) discussed security, and Ahmed Zaky explored serverless Powers of AWS. Together, they provided students with valuable skills and a strong foundation in cloud technology.
